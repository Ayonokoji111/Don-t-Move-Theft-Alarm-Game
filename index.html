HOW TO PLAY
01

Connect your phone in Nikki Streams and place it flat on a steady table.

02

Choose a time and press Start. Stay still during the short calibration.

03

Keep the phone still until time runs out. Any sustained movement ends the round.

Motion readings stay on your local network. This demo is for learning, not guaranteed theft protection.
